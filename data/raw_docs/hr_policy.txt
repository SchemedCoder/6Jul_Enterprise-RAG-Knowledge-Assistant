HR POLICY – EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Company: 6Jul Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Document Type: Human Resources Policy
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Policy Owner: Human Resources Department

1. PURPOSE

This HR Policy defines the company's general policies and guidelines relating to employee conduct, working hours, leave, attendance, compensation, benefits, remote work, performance management, and separation.

The purpose of this policy is to provide employees with clear and consistent information regarding workplace expectations and HR processes.

2. WORKING HOURS

The standard working schedule is Monday through Friday.

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, including a one-hour lunch break.

Employees are expected to be available during their assigned working hours unless they have approved leave, remote-work arrangements, or other authorization from their manager.

Employees working in shifts must follow the working hours assigned to them by their respective teams.

3. ATTENDANCE

Employees are expected to maintain regular and punctual attendance.

Employees who are unable to report to work due to an emergency or unexpected circumstance should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Repeated unauthorized absence or persistent lateness may result in corrective action in accordance with company policy.

4. ANNUAL LEAVE

Full-time employees are eligible for 18 days of annual paid leave per calendar year.

Employees should submit leave requests through the company's designated HR or employee-management system.

Planned leave should generally be requested at least three working days in advance.

Leave approval is subject to business requirements and manager approval.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward according to applicable company rules and local employment regulations.

5. SICK LEAVE

Employees who are unable to work due to illness should notify their manager at the earliest opportunity.

Employees may be required to provide appropriate medical documentation for extended periods of absence, subject to applicable law and company requirements.

6. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE

Eligible employees may receive maternity or paternity leave in accordance with applicable employment laws and company policy.

Employees should contact Human Resources before the planned leave period to understand eligibility, documentation, and approval requirements.

Where applicable, statutory requirements take precedence over this policy.

7. REMOTE WORK POLICY

Eligible employees may work remotely when approved by their manager and in accordance with the requirements of their role.

Employees working remotely are expected to:

- Maintain normal working hours.
- Remain reachable through approved communication channels.
- Attend scheduled meetings.
- Protect company information and confidential data.
- Maintain a suitable and secure working environment.
- Meet normal productivity and performance expectations.

Remote work is not an automatic entitlement and may be modified or withdrawn based on business requirements.

8. FLEXIBLE WORKING

Flexible working arrangements may be considered based on employee responsibilities, business requirements, team schedules, and manager approval.

Employees must ensure that flexible working arrangements do not negatively affect customer commitments, team collaboration, or business operations.

9. CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees are expected to behave professionally and respectfully toward colleagues, customers, vendors, and other stakeholders.

The company does not tolerate:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Workplace violence or threats.
- Bullying or intimidation.
- Fraud or deliberate misrepresentation.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
- Theft or misuse of company property.
- Conflicts of interest that are not disclosed appropriately.

Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, subject to applicable law and company procedures.

10. ANTI-HARASSMENT AND NON-DISCRIMINATION

The company is committed to maintaining a workplace free from harassment and unlawful discrimination.

Employment decisions should be based on legitimate business considerations, qualifications, performance, and applicable employment requirements.

Employees who experience or observe inappropriate conduct are encouraged to report the matter to Human Resources or an appropriate management representative.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY

Employees may have access to confidential company, employee, customer, financial, technical, or business information.

Confidential information must only be accessed, used, stored, or shared for legitimate business purposes.

Employees must not disclose confidential information to unauthorized individuals.

Confidentiality obligations may continue after an employee leaves the company.

12. INFORMATION SECURITY

Employees must follow company information-security requirements.

Employees must:

- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Protect company devices from unauthorized access.
- Use approved systems and applications.
- Report suspected security incidents promptly.
- Avoid sharing credentials with other individuals.
- Follow company policies regarding customer and employee data.

13. COMPANY EQUIPMENT

Company equipment provided to employees remains company property.

Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of company-issued laptops, mobile devices, access cards, and other equipment.

Lost, stolen, or damaged equipment should be reported to the appropriate IT or HR team as soon as possible.

Company equipment must normally be returned when employment ends or when requested by the company.

14. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

Employees may be reimbursed for reasonable and authorized business expenses.

Expenses must be submitted through the approved expense-management process and should include appropriate supporting documentation such as receipts.

Expenses that were not authorized or do not comply with company guidelines may not be reimbursed.

15. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

Employee performance may be reviewed periodically by managers.

Performance reviews may consider:

- Achievement of assigned objectives.
- Quality of work.
- Productivity.
- Collaboration and teamwork.
- Communication.
- Professional behavior.
- Skills development.
- Customer or stakeholder feedback.

Employees may receive feedback, development recommendations, or performance-improvement plans where appropriate.

16. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

The company encourages employees to develop their technical, professional, and leadership skills.

Training opportunities may include internal training, external courses, certifications, workshops, mentoring, and other approved learning programs.

Participation in company-sponsored training may require manager or HR approval.

17. PROMOTION

Promotions are based on factors including performance, demonstrated skills, experience, role requirements, business needs, and availability of suitable positions.

Completion of a specific length of service does not automatically guarantee promotion.

18. EMPLOYEE GRIEVANCES

Employees who have concerns regarding workplace conditions, management decisions, harassment, discrimination, compensation, or other employment matters may raise the issue with their manager or Human Resources.

Employees are encouraged to raise concerns promptly so that the company can review and address them appropriately.

19. DISCIPLINARY ACTION

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, disciplinary action may include:

- Verbal counseling.
- Written warning.
- Performance improvement plan.
- Suspension where applicable.
- Termination of employment.

The level of disciplinary action will depend on the nature and seriousness of the issue, previous conduct, and relevant company procedures.

20. RESIGNATION AND NOTICE PERIOD

Employees who wish to resign should submit a formal resignation through the company's designated process.

The applicable notice period will be based on the employee's employment agreement and applicable company policy.

Employees are expected to complete required knowledge transfer and return company property before their last working day.

21. EXIT PROCESS

Employees leaving the company must complete the applicable exit formalities.

Exit formalities may include:

- Knowledge transfer.
- Return of company equipment.
- Return of identification cards or access cards.
- Clearance from relevant departments.
- Completion of exit documentation.
- Final payroll and benefits processing.

22. FINAL SETTLEMENT

Final settlement may include eligible salary, approved reimbursements, accrued benefits, deductions, and other applicable payments.

The final settlement process is subject to completion of required clearance and applicable company procedures and employment laws.

23. EMPLOYEE DATA PRIVACY

Employee personal information must be collected, processed, stored, and accessed only for legitimate business, employment, legal, or administrative purposes.

Access to employee information should be limited to authorized personnel.

24. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest that could affect their ability to perform their duties objectively.

Employees should not use their position or company resources for unauthorized personal benefit.

25. POLICY CHANGES

The company reserves the right to modify, update, or replace HR policies based on business requirements, changes in applicable law, or organizational needs.

Employees are responsible for reviewing applicable policy updates communicated by the company.

26. POLICY QUESTIONS

Employees who have questions regarding HR policies should contact the Human Resources Department through the company's approved communication channel.
